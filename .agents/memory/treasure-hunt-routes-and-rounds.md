name: Treasure-hunt routes and rounds
description: Event rules for randomized POIs, shared final stops, and Round 2 selection.
---

All active checkpoints except two designated final stops are visited first in a randomized order. The same two designated checkpoints close every team's route; the number of randomized checkpoints can vary.

Round 2 participants are selected manually by volunteers, and the advancement count is undecided. Do not assume a fixed team quota. Do not add a same-day/separate-day setting unless the user asks.

**Why:** The user clarified that route length may vary, the final two locations are shared, volunteers choose Round 2 teams, and there is no confirmed qualification count or in-app schedule requirement.

**How to apply:** Keep route assignment based on active checkpoints and exactly two volunteer-designated final stops. Preserve prior-round routes and results when beginning Round 2.
